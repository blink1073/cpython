:mod:`smtplib`: :meth:`~smtplib.SMTP.auth_plain` now accepts Unicode
usernames and passwords when the server advertises the ``SMTPUTF8``
extension (:rfc:`6531`): credentials are encoded as UTF-8, and any embedded
NUL characters (``\x00``) are escaped as ``%x00`` to preserve the SASL
PLAIN field framing. :meth:`~smtplib.SMTP.auth` now selects UTF-8 encoding
automatically when ``SMTPUTF8`` is advertised, falling back to ASCII
otherwise. :meth:`~smtplib.SMTP.auth_login` and
:meth:`~smtplib.SMTP.auth_cram_md5` raise :exc:`UnicodeEncodeError` for
non-ASCII credentials, as those mechanisms are defined for ASCII only.
Unicode credential handling for SASL PLAIN is also tested in :mod:`imaplib`
(via ``ENABLE UTF8=ACCEPT``) and :mod:`ftplib`.
